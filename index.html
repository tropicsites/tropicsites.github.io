FAQs

Questions?
We've got answers.

Don't know anything about websites? That's completely fine.

Do I need to know anything about websites?

Nope. You don't need to understand code, hosting, DNS or anything technical. We'll handle that side and explain anything you need to know in plain English.

How long does a website take?

It depends on the size and complexity of the project. We'll give you a clear timeframe before we start.

Can you help me with Google?

Yes. We build websites with SEO fundamentals in place and can help with local search, Google Business Profile and ongoing optimisation.

Can you host my website?

Yes. We can help arrange reliable hosting and keep everything online after your website launches.

Can I update my website myself?

Absolutely. Depending on how your website is built, we can set it up so you can make your own changes, or we can look after updates for you.

Do you work with businesses outside Cairns?

Yes. We're based around the tropical north, but websites can be created for businesses anywhere in Australia.
